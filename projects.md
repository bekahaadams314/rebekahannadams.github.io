### [Temporal Respiratory Illness Dynamics](https://github.com/bekahaadams314/respiratory_illness_analysis)
Engineered features from over a decade of CDC respiratory-virus surveillance data, then trained an XGBoost regressor to forecast influenza test-positivity two weeks ahead, validating against a held-out flu season to confirm model generalizability. Achieved a validation RMSE of 0.0248 (~2.5%).

### [Operational Supply Chain Tail-Risk Simulation for Pharma Manufacturing](https://github.com/bekahaadams314/supply_chain_cvar_simulaton_for_pharma_manufacturing)
Developed a Python-based Monte Carlo simulation, using Pandas and SciPy, to quantify supply chain disruption risk, applying VaR/CVaR to determine tail risk exposure and demonstrate how the analysis could inform capital and operational planning decisions. Surfaced a $2.4M annual loss threshold at the 5th percentile.
